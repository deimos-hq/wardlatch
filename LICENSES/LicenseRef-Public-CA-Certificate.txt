Public CA certificates

lib/incidents/emudhra-2026-10-roots.pem holds two public root certificates,
copied unchanged:

  AAA Certificate Services (Comodo CA Limited)
  emSign ECC Root CA - G3 (eMudhra Technologies Limited)

Certificate authorities publish their root certificates so that anyone can
distribute them and use them to verify TLS connections; operating systems,
browsers and language runtimes all ship them. Wardlatch includes these two only
so its positive control can force them in and confirm that a probe is able to
see exposure (see README.md and ASSURANCE_CASE.md).
